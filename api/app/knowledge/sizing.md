# Sizing

## The short version

noszn is cut boxy. Tees and hoodies sit wide through the body with a dropped
shoulder and a shorter length. If you want the intended fit, take your usual
size. If you want it oversized, size up once.

## Known issue

The Boxy Tee runs about half a size small through the chest compared to the
rest of the range. It's the most common complaint we get and the most common
reason for an exchange. Sizing up one is the right call for most people.

The Cargo Pant runs true to waist but long in the leg — a 32" inseam on
everything up to L.

## Measurements

Garment measurements, laid flat, in inches.

| Size | Chest | Length | Shoulder |
|------|-------|--------|----------|
| XS   | 20    | 26     | 18       |
| S    | 21.5  | 27     | 19       |
| M    | 23    | 28     | 20       |
| L    | 24.5  | 29     | 21       |
| XL   | 26    | 30     | 22       |

Accessories — beanies and caps — are one size.

## Between sizes

Take the smaller one for tees and the larger one for outerwear. The Puffer
Vest is cut to go over a hoodie, so it already has the room built in.
