# Returns and exchanges

## The window

30 days from delivery. Pieces need to be unworn and unwashed with the tags
still on. We can't take back anything that's been worn out, washed, or that
comes back smelling of smoke or perfume.

## How it works

Email returns@noszn.example with the order number. We send a returns label:
£3.50 for UK, deducted from the refund. Refunds go back to the original
payment method within 5 working days of the parcel reaching us.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are free in the UK — we cover the label both ways. This is on
purpose. Sizing is the single biggest reason anything comes back, and we would
much rather someone ends up with the size that fits than gets a refund and
doesn't come back.

If the size you want has sold out by the time your exchange reaches us, we
refund in full and let you know.

## Sale and archive

Anything bought in a sale or from the archive is final. It says so on the
product page.

## Faulty pieces

If something arrives faulty or with a fault that shows up in normal wear, we
cover it regardless of the 30 days. Send a photo to the same address and we
replace it or refund it, including postage both ways.
