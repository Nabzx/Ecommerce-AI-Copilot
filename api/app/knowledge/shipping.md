# Shipping

## UK

Standard delivery is £3.95, or free on orders over £75. Orders are picked and
dispatched within 1 working day, and Royal Mail Tracked 48 usually lands 2–4
working days after that.

Express delivery is £7.95 with Royal Mail Tracked 24, ordered before 2pm on a
working day, delivered the next working day.

## Europe

£12 flat to EU countries, 5–9 working days. Duties are paid by the customer on
delivery — we don't collect them at checkout, so the courier may ask for a
payment before handing the parcel over.

## Rest of world

£18 flat, 7–14 working days. Same duties situation as Europe.

## Drops

Drop orders are the exception. When a drop lands we take more orders in a day
than we normally take in a fortnight, so dispatch moves to 3–5 working days
while we get through the queue. The drop page says so at checkout.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed the moment the label is printed. It can take up to
24 hours before the courier scans the parcel and the link starts showing
movement, which is the single most common thing people message us about.

## Missing parcels

Royal Mail won't open an investigation until 10 working days after the due
date for UK orders, or 20 for international. Once that point is reached we
send a replacement if we still have the size, or refund in full.
