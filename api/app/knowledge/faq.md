# FAQ

## When is the next drop?

Drops are announced on the mailing list first, usually a week ahead, then on
Instagram two days out. There's no fixed schedule — roughly four a year.

## Will sold-out sizes come back?

Core pieces (the Core Hoodie, Boxy Tee, Cargo Pant) get restocked. Seasonal
and collaboration pieces don't — when they're gone they're gone. Restocks are
announced on the mailing list.

## Can I cancel or change an order?

Only before it's dispatched, which is usually within a few hours. Email as
soon as possible with the order number. Once the label is printed it has to go
through the returns process instead.

## Do you ship internationally?

Yes, worldwide. Duties are the customer's responsibility on anything going
outside the UK.

## What's the fabric?

Hoodies and crewnecks are 400gsm brushed-back cotton loopback. Tees are 240gsm
combed cotton. Cargos are 100% cotton ripstop. Everything is cut and sewn in
Portugal.

## How should I wash it?

Cold wash, inside out, hang dry. The heavyweight cotton will shrink slightly
in a tumble dryer and the print will crack if it goes in hot.

## Do you restock sold-out sizes quickly?

Restocks run on roughly a two-month cycle because of the production lead time.
If a size sells out early in that cycle it stays gone for a while, which is the
main reason people give up and buy elsewhere.

## Is there a student discount?

10% with a valid student email, applied at checkout.
